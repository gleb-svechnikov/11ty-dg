# Where to Publish: Venues for the Teacher Knowledge Management Articles

*Prepared 21 October 2026. Deadlines and policies change, so check every entry on the official site before submitting. Items marked "unconfirmed" are inferred from earlier years' patterns.*

## The articles and their best fit

| Article | What it is | Best kind of venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Teacher's Knowledge Management Workflow** | A practitioner workflow (capture → distil → connect → align → plan → reflect → publish) | Practitioner magazines, experience-report tracks, teacher conferences |
| **Concepts First, Curricula Second: A Teacher's Knowledge Graph** | A conceptual model; compared with Marble's os-taxonomy | Computing-education conferences, education journals (position or theory paper) |
| **From Teacher's Notes to a Professional Digital Garden** | Digital garden publishing and PLN, plus a study protocol | Preprint now; a full paper once pilot data exists |

None of the three contains empirical results yet. Peer-reviewed research tracks will expect data, so the realistic first targets are **practitioner venues, experience reports, posters and preprints**. Section 4 of the workflow article (the pilot study) is the route to a later journal paper.

## 1. Practitioner magazines (fastest, widest teacher audience)

### Hello World (Raspberry Pi Foundation and Computing at School)
- Free magazine about computing education, written by educators, for educators.
- To submit, you email the editorial team with an idea. There is no formal call.
- **Fit:** excellent for a 1–3 page version of the *Workflow* article, focused on the reflect → update-concept loop and one concrete example (*Algorithm* across Grade 3 and Grade 10).
- Check the current editorial contact on helloworld.cc.

## 3. Journals

| Journal | Access / cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discover Education** (Springer) | Open access, so expect an article processing charge | Has a call on *teacher professional development and educational quality in changing learning environments*, open **29 Jul 2026 – 29 Apr 2027**. Best current match for the Workflow article |
| **Professional Development in Education** (Taylor & Francis) | Hybrid: free to publish, with paid optional open access | Teacher professional learning. Likely to need data, so better after the pilot |
| **Computers and Education Open** (Elsevier) | Open access, APC about USD 1,300 (aggregator figure, confirm with Elsevier) | Technology in education. Knowledge Graph plus pilot results |
| **Journal of Interactive Media in Education (JIME)** | Diamond open access, CC licence | **Currently closed to general submissions** and accepting only special collections. Focuses on higher and post-compulsory education, so a weaker fit. Watch for a relevant collection |
| **Journal of Knowledge Management** (Emerald) | Hybrid | Business/organisational KM. Only worth it for a theory-heavy version of the PKM argument. No open calls right now |

**Caution:** search results also turn up low-quality and predatory "international journals" with fast acceptance and fees. Before submitting anywhere, check that the journal is indexed in DOAJ, Scopus or Web of Science and has a real editorial board.

## 4. Preprints and permanent records (do this first)

- **EdArXiv:** a free, non-profit preprint server for education research, part of the Open Science Framework (OSF) network. You need an OSF account. It gives the article a citable identifier and a timestamp, and most conferences and journals accept submissions that already have a preprint (check each policy).
- **Zenodo:** gives a DOI for any version of the articles. It also suits the template knowledge base (the Markdown files), which matches the articles' open-format argument.

## 5. Self-publishing and community channels

- **Your own digital garden:** publishing the three articles on the garden itself demonstrates the workflow they describe. Link the preprint DOIs from there.
- **Long-form platforms** (Substack, Medium, dev.to): reach teachers and the PKM community. Cross-post a shorter version and link to the full article.
- **Communities:** Obsidian forum (Share & Showcase), Computing at School community, CSTA community, and the PKM / tools-for-thought communities. Good for feedback before a formal submission.

## Suggested sequence

1. **Now (Oct 2026):** post all three articles to EdArXiv or Zenodo and publish them on the garden. Pitch a short *Workflow* piece to **Hello World**. If the CSTA 2027 proposal window is still open, submit a workshop proposal.
2. **Winter 2026–27:** submit the *Knowledge Graph* article as a poster or short paper to **ITiCSE 2027**, then **WiPSCE 2027** in the spring.
3. **By 29 Apr 2027:** submit the *Workflow* article to the **Discover Education** call on teacher professional development.
4. **2027 school year:** run the pilot study. Use the results for a full paper (Computers and Education Open, Professional Development in Education) and an experience report at **SIGCSE TS 2028** or **CEP 2028**.
